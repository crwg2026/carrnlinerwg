Life Insurance Brokerage

Protection that fits your life — not a one-size-fits-all policy

As a brokerage, we help you compare types of coverage and understand the trade-offs before you decide. Here’s the plain-language breakdown most families wish someone had given them sooner.

⚡

Term Life

Best for: Maximum coverage for a set period — often 10, 20, or 30 years — at the lowest initial cost.

Covers the years your family needs it most: mortgage, raising kids, income replacement. Pure protection, no cash value.

Think of it as: Renting protection for a season of life.

📈

Indexed Universal Life (IUL)

Best for: Lifelong coverage plus cash-value growth potential linked to a market index — without direct market losses when the index falls (subject to caps, participation rates, and policy charges).

Flexible premiums, potential tax-advantaged access to cash value, and a death benefit for your family.

Think of it as: Protection + a long-term wealth-building tool in one policy.

🌳

Whole Life

Best for: Permanent coverage with guaranteed cash-value growth and level premiums for life.

Often used for final expenses, legacy giving, and certainty — the policy is designed to stay in force for your whole life as long as premiums are paid.

Think of it as: Owning permanent protection.

Retirement Planning & Annuities

Retirement income you don’t have to guess at.

Annuities are insurance products designed to help turn savings into income — in some cases, income guaranteed for life. They’re not right for everyone, and the details matter: payout options, fees, surrender periods, and how an annuity fits with Social Security and other savings.

  • Fixed annuities — predictable, guaranteed rate for a set period
  • Fixed indexed annuities — growth potential linked to an index, with principal protection from market downturns (per contract terms)
  • Income planning — mapping Social Security, savings, and annuities into one monthly picture

Questions families ask us

Do I need life insurance if I already have it through work?

Work coverage is a great start, but it’s usually 1–2× your salary, it may not be enough to cover a mortgage and years of income, and it typically doesn’t follow you if you change jobs. A personal policy you own stays with you. We can review what you have — free — and tell you honestly if it’s enough.

What’s the difference between an IUL and a 401(k) or IRA?

They do different jobs. A 401(k)/IRA is a retirement savings/investment account. An IUL is a life insurance policy first — it provides a death benefit — with cash value that can grow based on an index and may be accessed tax-advantaged under current rules. Many families use both. We’ll explain how they can work together, including the costs and limits of each.

I’m a first-time buyer. Where do I actually start?

Start with a conversation, not a listing app. We’ll look at your budget comfort, credit picture, down-payment options (including assistance programs you may qualify for), and timeline — then connect the home search to a plan you can afford long-term.

Are annuities safe? Can I lose money?

Fixed and fixed indexed annuities are insurance contracts — guarantees are backed by the issuing insurance company and spelled out in the contract. They typically protect principal from market downturns but have surrender periods and caps/fees that affect growth. That’s exactly why we review the full contract details with you before any decision.
